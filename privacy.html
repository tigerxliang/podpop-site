PodPop Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 15, 2026

PodPop is a language-learning app that plays public podcasts (Spanish, English and Chinese) with subtitles and marks words you may not know. We built it so that your learning data stays on your phone.

What we collect

Very little. You can use PodPop without an account, and your learning data stays on your device. We only store data on our server when you choose to create an account, share an episode, or post in the community, as described below. We never sell personal information or use it for advertising or tracking, and there is no analytics, advertising or tracking SDK in the app.

Your account (optional)

You can sign in with Apple or verify an email address in Me > Profile. If you do, we store on our server (hosted by Cloudflare): an account ID, the anonymous user identifier that Apple provides, and the email address you verified (or the email Apple shares with your permission; if you use Apple's Hide My Email, we do not keep that relay address). This is used only to sign you in. Verification codes are emailed to you through our email provider, Resend. You can unlink a sign-in method, or delete your account at any time under Me > Profile > Delete Account, which permanently removes this data from our server.

Sharing an episode

When you share an episode, the app uploads that episode's subtitles, word glosses and word highlights to our server so the shared link can open a web page. It does not include your name, email or other personal information. The page can be viewed by anyone who has the link.

Community

Posts and comments you choose to publish in the community are stored in Apple's iCloud public database together with an anonymous iCloud user identifier, and may be visible to other users. You can delete your own posts.

Subscriptions

PodPop offers a 7-day free trial and then an optional monthly subscription. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or receive your payment details or Apple Account information; the app only asks the App Store whether your subscription is active.

Data stored on your device

The following is stored on your device: your subscriptions, your native and learning language settings, the words you mark or remove, your listening statistics, and the optional nickname and avatar in your profile. Except as described above, it is never sent to us or to any server we operate.

iCloud sync

On iOS, PodPop can sync your marked words, subscriptions, language settings, listening statistics, nickname and avatar between your own devices through Apple's iCloud, using the Apple ID signed in on your device. This data is stored in your private iCloud storage under Apple's privacy policy; the developer cannot see or access it. Your account email is not synced through iCloud. You can turn iCloud Sync off at any time under Me > Profile.

Subtitles created on your device

For shows that do not publish subtitles, PodPop can create them with Apple's on-device speech recognition. The episode audio is downloaded from the publisher to your device, recognized on the device, and kept in a local cache. No audio or text is uploaded.

Content from third parties

Podcast feeds, audio, subtitles and cover images are loaded directly from each podcast publisher's own public servers, the same way any podcast app works. Those publishers may see standard request information such as your IP address, under their own privacy policies. PodPop does not control that content.

Permissions

Photo library — only if you choose a profile picture. The selected image is kept on your device.
Background audio — so episodes keep playing when the screen is locked.
Motion — only to detect when you turn the phone, so subtitles can switch between portrait and landscape. Motion data is not stored.

Children

PodPop is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.

PodPop 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 15 日

PodPop 是一款语言学习应用：播放公开的西班牙语、英语、中文播客和字幕，并标出你可能不认识的单词。我们的设计原则是：你的学习数据只留在你的手机上。

我们收集什么

很少。不注册账号也能使用 PodPop，你的学习数据保存在你的设备上。只有在你主动注册账号、转发节目或在社区发帖时，我们才会在服务器上保存相应的数据，详见下文。我们不会出售个人信息，也不会把它用于广告或追踪，应用内没有任何统计、广告或追踪工具。

账号（可选）

你可以在“我的 > 个人资料”里通过 Apple 登录或验证邮箱。这样做时，我们会在服务器（托管在 Cloudflare）上保存：账号编号、Apple 提供的匿名用户标识，以及你验证过的邮箱（或你允许 Apple 共享给我们的邮箱；如果你使用 Apple 的“隐藏邮件地址”，我们不会保留那个转发地址）。这些信息只用于登录。验证码通过我们的邮件服务商 Resend 发送到你的邮箱。你可以随时解除某种登录方式，或在“我的 > 个人资料 > 删除账号”里删除账号，这些数据会从我们的服务器上永久删除。

转发节目

当你转发一集节目时，应用会把这一集的字幕、单词释义和单词标记上传到我们的服务器，让转发的链接能打开网页版播放页。其中不包含你的姓名、邮箱或其他个人信息。拿到链接的人都可以查看这个页面。

用户社区

你在社区里主动发布的帖子和评论，会和一个匿名的 iCloud 用户标识一起保存在苹果 iCloud 的公共数据库里，其他用户可能看到。你可以删除自己发布的内容。

订阅

PodPop 提供 7 天免费体验，之后可按月订阅。购买完全由 Apple 通过 App Store 处理，我们看不到也不会收到你的付款信息或 Apple 账户信息；应用只会向 App Store 查询你的订阅是否有效。

保存在你设备上的数据

以下内容保存在你的设备上：订阅、母语和学习语言设置、标记或移除的单词、学习统计，以及你在个人资料里设置的昵称和头像。除上文所述外，这些信息不会发送给我们，也不会上传到我们运营的任何服务器。

iCloud 同步

在 iOS 上，PodPop 可以通过苹果 iCloud（使用你设备上登录的 Apple ID），在你自己的设备之间同步标记的单词、订阅、语言设置、学习统计、昵称和头像。这些数据保存在你的 iCloud 私人空间里，适用苹果的隐私政策，开发者看不到、也无法访问。账号邮箱不会通过 iCloud 同步。你可以随时在“我的 > 个人资料”里关闭 iCloud 同步。

在设备上生成的字幕

对于没有发布字幕的节目，PodPop 可以用苹果的本机语音识别生成字幕：节目音频从发布方下载到你的设备，在设备上识别，并保存在本地缓存里。不会上传任何音频或文字。

来自第三方的内容

播客节目列表、音频、字幕和封面图直接从各播客发布方自己的公开服务器加载，和所有播客应用一样。发布方可能看到 IP 地址等常规请求信息，适用其各自的隐私政策。PodPop 不控制这些内容。

权限

相册：仅在你设置头像时使用，所选图片只保存在本机。
后台音频：锁屏后继续播放。
运动传感器：仅用于判断你是否转动了手机，以便字幕在横屏和竖屏之间切换，不保存任何运动数据。
